About

Engineering intelligence at the edge

Benguerir, Morocco
Mohammed VI Polytechnic University

I am an embedded electronics researcher and R&D engineer focused on turning advanced algorithms into efficient, reliable and practical hardware–software systems.

My work combines electronic system design, embedded programming and machine learning for applications in biomedical monitoring, assisted diagnosis, industrial systems and intelligent connected devices. Alongside research, I teach electronics and embedded systems, supervise student projects and support prototyping and makerspace laboratories.
